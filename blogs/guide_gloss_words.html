📖 The Sacred Pilgrimage Lexicon

Hajj & Umrah — Terms, Rituals & Spiritual Meanings
✨ 69 essential entries from classical sources
🕋 Comprehensive reference — from Adhan to Zamzam
Includes rites: Ifrad, Qiran, Tamattu‘, Tawaf, Rami, Waqfah and more.
📜 Based on authentic Islamic tradition
Adhan #1
The call to Prayer (salah), usually issued from the minaret of a mosque.
Arafah / Arafat #2
A desert location approximately 14.5 km (9 miles) east of Makkah where the pilgrim spends 9 Dhul-Hijjah as a rite of Hajj. The waqfah is performed at `Arafah.
Damm #3
Also known as kaffarah. The atonement required of a pilgrim for a willful violation of a prohibition or obligation while in the state of ihram.
Day of An-Nahr (Yawm An-Nahr) #4
This day, 10 Dhul-Hijjah, is designated as the preferred day of sacrifice during Hajj.
Day of At-Tarwiyah #5
The start of the Hajj, on 8 Dhul-Hijjah. The pilgrim proceeds to Mina on this day.
Days of Tashreeq (Ayyam At-Tashreeq) #6
These are 11, 12, and 13 Dhul-Hijjah. On these days, the pilgrim throws pebbles at the stone pillars, a ritual known as rami, in Mina. The name Tashreeq, related to the word for sunrise, comes from the practice in the past of drying the meat of the sacrifices in the sun on those days.
Dhul-Hijjah #7
The last month of the Islamic calendar.
Eid Al-Adha #8
"The Islamic public celebration of sacrifice performed on 10 Dhul-Hijjah. An animal such as a sheep or goat is sacrificed as a commemoration of Prophet Ibrahim's (peace and blessings be upon him) willingness to sacrifice his son Isma`il for Allah."
Hadi #9
Hadi is a sacrificial animal immolated in the name of Allah by the pilgrim. It is to be slaughtered in Al-Haram and distributed among the poor and the needy.
Hajj #10
One of the five pillars or central duties of Islam. It is a set of acts of worship to be performed in and around Makkah at least once in a lifetime by every Muslim satisfying certain conditions. There are three types of Hajj: ifrad, qiran and tamattu` (see individual entries).
Al-Hajar Al-Aswad (Black Stone) #11
The Black Stone built into the southeast corner of the Ka`bah at a height of approximately four feet. The stone does not belong to the geology of the region and is a part of the original construction of the Ka`bah by Prophet Ibrahim (peace be upon him). Touching and kissing Hajar Al-Aswad during `Umrah and Hajj is considered sunnah.
Halq #12
The complete shaving of the head by the male pilgrim on the 10th of Dhul-Hijjah. This is the last thing he does before getting out of the state of ihram. For female pilgrims, the requirement is satisfied if they trim their hair by approximately half an inch.
Al-Haram Ash-Shareef #13
"The mosque around the Ka`bah in Makkah, also known as Al-Masjid Al-Haram or just Al-Haram, as well as the mosque in Madinah. The latter, also known as Al-Masjid An-Nabawi (the Prophet's Mosque), contains within its premises the grave of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him)."
Hateem #14
The area adjacent to the Ka`bah on its west side, enclosed by a low semi-circular wall. Tradition has it that Hajar (wife of Prophet Ibrahim) is buried in this enclosure. It is highly recommended that the pilgrim offer sunnah Prayer and supplications to Allah in this area.
Idtiba' #15
The mode of ihram used during Tawaf Al-Qudum. The male pilgrim drapes one end of the top part of his ihram over his left shoulder back-to-front. Female pilgrims wear no ihram, so the question of idtiba` for them does not arise.
Ifrad #16
The type of Hajj in which the pilgrim pronounces his intention to perform only Hajj (as opposed to Hajj and `Umrah) at the miqat while changing into ihram.
Ihram #17
(1) The distinctive garb of the male pilgrim: two pieces of white, plain and unsewn cloth. (2) The state of consecration. While in ihram, the pilgrim may not use perfume, engage in sexual intercourse, clip nails, or cut hair.
Ihsar #18
Al-Ihsar (as used in the Quran) means blocking the path to the House of Allah. Allah says, (And complete the Hajj or `Umrah in the service of Allah. But if you are prevented (from completing it) send an offering for sacrifice.) (Al-Baqarah 2:196)
Istilam #19
The act of kissing the Black Stone (Al-Hajar Al-Aswad) at the beginning and the end of every circuit of the Ka`bah during Tawaf. If not possible, the pilgrim may extend his hand to touch the Stone and then kiss his own hand.
Izar #20
Lower cloth of the male pilgrim's clothes.
Jamarat #21
The three stone pillars in Mina that symbolically represent the locations where the devil (Shaytan) tried to tempt Prophet Ibrahim. The pilgrim stones these pillars on the 10th through 13th of Dhul-Hijjah.
Al-Jamrah Al-Kubra #22
The last and biggest stone pillar in the line. This is also called Jamrat Al-Aqabah.
Al-Jamrah Al-Ula #23
The first stone pillar in the line.
Al-Jamrah Al-Wusta #24
The second (middle) stone pillar in the line.
Ka'bah #25
A cubic structure originally built by Prophet Ibrahim and his eldest son Isma`il. It is now housed within Al-Haram Ash-Shareef in Makkah. During Hajj and `Umrah, the essential rite of Tawaf is performed around the Ka`bah.
Kaffarah #26
Another name for damm. The atonement required of a pilgrim for a willful violation of a prohibition or obligation while in the state of ihram.
Kiswah #27
The black cloth that drapes the Ka`bah. It is embroidered in gold thread with the Shahadah, praises of Allah, and verses of the Quran.
Mabrur #28
Accepted. A Hajj mabrur is one that is accepted by Allah. The sign of its acceptance is that a person will go back in a better state than when he came, and discontinue the sins that were between him and Allah.
Marwah #29
A rocky hillock located approximately one hundred yards (91 meters) from the Ka`bah inside Al-Masjid Al-Haram. The pilgrim performs the devotional rite of Sa`i between the hillocks of Safa and Marwah.
Mahram #30
The husband or close male relative of a woman to whom marriage is expressly prohibited by the Shari`ah (e.g., father, grandfather, brother, uncle, nephew).
Maqam Ibrahim #31
The stepping stone used by Prophet Ibrahim during the original construction of the Ka`bah. The stone carries the imprints of his feet and is housed in a glass enclosure on the north side of the Ka`bah.
Al-Masjid Al-Haram #32
The mosque around the Ka`bah, also known as Al-Haram or Al-Haram Ash-Shareef.
Mas'aa #33
The distance between Safa and Marwah. See also Sa`i.
Miqat #34
An imaginary boundary around Makkah. A prospective pilgrim cannot cross this boundary without first changing into ihram. The pilgrim changes into ihram at or before the miqat.
Mina #35
A desert location approximately three miles (4.8 km) east of Makkah where several rites of Hajj are performed.
Mutawwif #36
A knowledgeable professional who can guide the pilgrim during Hajj. Also called a mu`allim.
Muhrim #37
A pilgrim in the state of ihram.
Multazam #38
The part of the Ka`bah between its door and the Black Stone. It is recommended that, if possible, the pilgrim touch the Ka`bah at Multazam and offer supplications to Allah.
Mutamatti #39
One who is performing or has performed tamattu` Hajj.
Mutawwif (Mu'allim) #40
A knowledgeable professional who can guide the pilgrim during Hajj. Also called a mu`allim.
Muzdalifah #41
A desert location approximately midway between Mina and `Arafah. The pilgrim spends the night of the 10th of Dhul-Hijjah there.
Namirah #42
A mosque in `Arafah.
Niyyah #43
Intention. All acts of worship are preceded by an appropriate niyyah.
Qarin #44
One who is performing or has performed qiran Hajj.
Qasr #45
The mode of shortened Prayers usually offered when on a journey.
Qiblah #46
The direction (towards the Ka`bah) that Muslims face when performing ritual Prayers (salah).
Qiran #47
The type of Hajj in which the pilgrim pronounces his intention to perform both `Umrah and Hajj together with the same ihram at the miqat.
Ramal #48
The ritual in which male pilgrims are required to walk briskly with their chests thrust forward during the first three circuits of Tawaf Al-Qudum. Women are not required to perform ramal.
Ridaa #49
The upper piece of ihram.
Rami #50
"The act of symbolically stoning the devil (Shaytan) in Mina on 10 through 13 Dhul-Hijjah. This commemorates the tradition that Prophet Ibrahim was tempted three times by the devil but rejected all attempts by stoning."
Safa #52
A small hillock approximately half a mile (0.8 km) from the Ka`bah, inside Al-Masjid Al-Haram. The pilgrim performs the ritual of Sa`i between Safa and Marwah.
Sa'i #53
The ritual of walking seven times back and forth between the rocky hillocks of Safa and Marwah. This retraces the footsteps of Hajar during her desperate search for water.
Salah #54
Ritual Prayers. The obligatory Prayers are performed five times a day.
Shawt #55
One complete circumambulation, or circuit, of the Ka`bah. Each circuit starts and ends at the Black Stone. Seven circuits constitute one Tawaf.
Talbiyah #56
"A recital of the words: Labbayka, Allahumma, labbayk... The Talbiyah is a prayer as well as an assertion of the pilgrim's conviction that he intends to perform Hajj only for the glory of Allah."
Tamattu' #57
The type of Hajj in which the pilgrim pronounces his intention to perform only `Umrah at the miqat, then performs `Umrah and goes out of ihram until the 8th of Dhul-Hijjah, when he makes a second intention and changes into ihram for Hajj.
Taqseer #58
Shortening or clipping of the whole head of hair by the male pilgrim following the completion of Hajj. This may be performed in lieu of halq (shaving).
Tawaf #59
The seven-circuit circumambulation of the Ka`bah while reciting prayers. It constitutes an integral part of `Umrah and Hajj.
Tawaf Al-Ifadah #60
The Tawaf performed by the pilgrim on the 10th of Dhul-Hijjah as the last formal rite of Hajj in Makkah after changing into street clothes. Also called Tawaf Az-Ziyarah.
Tawaf An-Nafl #61
A devotional Tawaf that may be performed any time.
Tawaf Al-Qudum #62
The initial Tawaf performed by the pilgrim upon entering Al-Masjid Al-Haram in Makkah pursuant upon his intention for Hajj.
Tawaf Al-`Umrah #63
The Tawaf performed as a rite of `Umrah.
Tawaf Al-Wada #64
The Farewell Tawaf performed by the pilgrim just before leaving Makkah for his next destination.
`Umrah #65
A set of religious and devotional rites performed in Makkah. `Umrah, or lesser Hajj, can be performed at any time of the year and, unlike Hajj, does not involve the rites at Mina, Muzdalifah, and `Arafah.
Waqfah #66
The ritual of standing before Allah at the Mount of Mercy in `Arafah. It is a central rite of Hajj.
Wudu #67
The ablution that is performed prior to ritual Prayer.
Ziyarah #68
"A visit to the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah, usually before or after hajj or `Umrah. It is not a part of the Hajj or `Umrah rites, but many pilgrims include this visit."
Zamzam #69
"The spring of water that sprang forth miraculously under the feet of the infant Isma`il (peace be upon him) during his mother's frantic search for water between Safa and Marwah. Zamzam is now enclosed in a marble chamber in the Ka'bah."
