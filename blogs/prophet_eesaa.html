🌟 Prophet 'Eesaa (Jesus) ﷺ

The Messiah, Son of Mary — a spirit from Allah, a sign for all worlds, and a mercy to humanity. His miraculous birth, his message of pure monotheism, and his glorious elevation to heaven.

📖 “Indeed, I am the servant of Allah. He has given me the Scripture and made me a prophet.” — Quran 19:30

No other prophet has stirred as much love, reverence, and theological debate as 'Eesaa ibn Maryam (Jesus, son of Mary, peace be upon him). In Islam, he is not a god, nor the son of God, but one of the greatest messengers of Allah — born of a virgin, given the ability to speak in the cradle, heal the blind and lepers, and raise the dead by Allah’s permission. His story, told with breathtaking detail in the Quran, is a bridge between divine power and human humility. This blog recounts his full narrative from an Islamic perspective: from the angel’s announcement to his ascension, and the profound lessons for every believer.

✨ The Annunciation: “When the angels said: 'O Mary, indeed Allah gives you good news of a word from Him, whose name will be the Messiah, Jesus, the son of Mary – honored in this world and the Hereafter and among those brought near [to Allah].'”
— Surah Aal-Imran (3:45)

🌿 The Miraculous Birth of 'Eesaa

Maryam (Mary), may Allah exalt her mention, was a pious woman from a noble family, raised in the temple under the care of Prophet Zakariyya (Zachariah). One day, while she was secluded in prayer, an angel appeared to her in the form of a man. Terrified, she sought refuge in the Most Merciful. The angel declared: “I am only the messenger of your Lord to give you a pure boy.” She asked: “How can I have a boy while no man has touched me and I have not been unchaste?” The reply came: “Thus it will be; your Lord says: 'It is easy for Me, and We will make him a sign to the people and a mercy from Us.'” (19:18-21).

Maryam conceived the child by Allah’s direct command — a miracle as great as the creation of Adam from dust. When the pains of childbirth drove her to a dry palm tree in solitude, she cried out in anguish: “Oh, I wish I had died before this and was in oblivion, forgotten.” Then a voice called from beneath her: “Do not grieve; your Lord has provided beneath you a stream. And shake toward you the trunk of the palm tree; it will drop upon you ripe, fresh dates.” (19:24-25).

🗣️ The Infant Speaks: Defending His Mother's Honor

Returning to her people with the newborn, Maryam was met with accusations and scandal. But Allah had already prepared a miracle. When they rebuked her, she pointed to the child. They exclaimed: “How can we speak to a newborn baby?” Then the infant 'Eesaa spoke clearly, saying: “Indeed, I am the servant of Allah. He has given me the Scripture and made me a prophet. And He has made me blessed wherever I am and has enjoined upon me prayer and Zakaah as long as I remain alive, and dutiful to my mother, and He has not made me a wretched tyrant. And peace is on me the day I was born and the day I will die and the day I am raised alive.” (19:30-33). This silenced the accusers and established his prophethood from infancy.

💡 Profound insight: The speech of infant 'Eesaa is a clear sign that his mission was divinely ordained. It also elevated Maryam’s status as the purest of women, chosen above all women of her time (Quran 3:42).

⚕️ The Prophetic Mission: Call to Monotheism & Miracles

As 'Eesaa grew, the signs of prophethood multiplied. He could tell his companions what they had eaten and what they had stored in their homes. He healed the blind and the leper, and by Allah’s leave, raised the dead. The Quran mentions that he also fashioned a bird from clay, breathed into it, and it became a living bird — all by Allah’s permission. He came to confirm the Torah that preceded him and to make lawful some of what had been forbidden to the Children of Israel. His core message was simple: “Worship Allah, my Lord and your Lord.”

He confronted the hypocrisy of the Pharisees and Sadducees, who had turned religion into a marketplace. He taught that righteousness is not in outward rituals alone, but in purity of heart, charity, and sincere devotion to the One God. When they brought him an adulteress, expecting him to uphold the stoning law, he responded: “Whoever among you is sinless can stone her.” They left one by one, unable to cast the first stone.

🍽️ The Heavenly Table Spread (Al-Ma'idah)

One of the greatest miracles requested by his disciples was a table spread with food from heaven. They said: “Can your Lord send down to us a table from heaven?” 'Eesaa warned them to fear Allah, but they insisted: “We wish to eat from it and let our hearts be reassured and know that you have been truthful to us.” So 'Eesaa prayed: “O Allah, our Lord! Send down to us a table from heaven to be for us a festival for the first of us and the last of us and a sign from You.” Allah responded: “Indeed, I will send it down to you, but whoever disbelieves afterwards – I will punish him with a punishment by which I have not punished anyone among the worlds.” (5:112-115). The table descended with abundant food, and thousands ate from it, yet it never depleted — a clear sign of Allah’s power and a warning against ingratitude.

📖 And [beware the Day] when Allah will say: “O 'Eesaa, Son of Maryam! Did you say to the people: 'Take me and my mother as deities besides Allah?'” He will say: “Exalted are You! It was not for me to say that to which I have no right. If I had said it, You would have known it...”
— Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:116)

⚔️ The Conspiracy to Kill 'Eesaa & Allah's Perfect Plan

The Jewish authorities felt threatened. The Sanhedrin plotted to kill 'Eesaa. One of the disciples, Judas Iscariot, betrayed him for thirty pieces of silver. They convinced the Roman governor that 'Eesaa was a threat to the empire and demanded his crucifixion. But the Quran declares with absolute certainty: “And they did not kill him, nor did they crucify him; but another was made to resemble him to them. And indeed, those who differ over it are in doubt about it. They have no knowledge of it except the following of assumption. And they did not kill him, for certain. Rather, Allah raised him to Himself. And ever is Allah Exalted in Might and Wise.” (4:157-158).

Allah saved His beloved prophet from the humiliation of the cross and raised him bodily to heaven. 'Eesaa will return before the end of time as a sign of the Hour, break the cross, kill the swine, and establish justice on earth, affirming the final message of Islam and the prophethood of Muhammad (ﷺ).

📜 Core Beliefs About 'Eesaa in Islam

  • A noble prophet and messenger — one of the five greatest (Ulu al-‘Azm) prophets.
  • Born of the virgin Maryam — a miracle from Allah, not a divine incarnation.
  • Not crucified nor killed — raised to heaven by Allah, and will return.
  • Not the son of God, nor God himself — he was a servant and worshipper of Allah.
  • Performed miracles only by Allah’s permission — to affirm his prophethood.
  • His message was pure monotheism (Tawheed) — worship Allah alone.
🌟 The Truth About the Messiah: Allah says: “They have certainly disbelieved who say, 'Allah is the Messiah, the son of Mary'… But the Messiah said, 'O Children of Israel, worship Allah, my Lord and your Lord.'” (5:72). Loving 'Eesaa means believing exactly what he preached: “Indeed, Allah is my Lord and your Lord, so worship Him. That is a straight path.”

📖 Surah Al-Ikhlas: The Ultimate Clarification

To leave no room for confusion, Allah revealed Surah Al-Ikhlas (112), which encapsulates the Islamic creed about God: “Say: He is Allah, [who is] One. Allah, the Eternal Refuge. He neither begets nor is born. Nor is there to Him any equivalent.” This chapter refutes all claims of divine fatherhood or sonship, including the deification of 'Eesaa or his mother. The heavens would almost rupture at such a claim, but Allah is far above what they associate with Him.

🤲 Lessons from the Life of 'Eesaa (PBUH)

  • Miracles do not make one divine — they are signs from Allah, not attributes of godhood.
  • Purity of lineage and heart — Maryam's chastity and devotion set the highest example for all believers.
  • Humility in leadership — 'Eesaa washed his disciples' feet and lived simply, never claiming lordship.
  • The importance of sincere disciples — even among the twelve, one betrayed, but the faithful remnant was saved.
  • Allah’s plan always prevails — the crucifixion plot failed, and Allah raised 'Eesaa to Himself.
🤍 Our Faith in 'Eesaa (PBUH)
We love and honor Prophet Jesus as a mighty messenger of Allah, the Messiah, a word from Allah cast upon Maryam, and a spirit from Him. We reject all false claims of his divinity or crucifixion. We await his blessed return, when he will break the cross, kill the pig, abolish the jizya, and rule by the Quran, confirming the finality of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ). May Allah’s peace be upon him, his pure mother, and all the prophets.
