Ayat e Karima

A powerful Quranic dua for forgiveness, mercy, and relief from hardships

لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ

Transliteration: La ilaha illa anta, subhanaka inni kuntu minaz-zalimeen

English: "There is no god but You. Glory be to You! Indeed, I was among the wrongdoers."

Urdu: اے اللہ! تیرے سوا کوئی معبود نہیں، تو پاک ہے، بے شک میں ظالموں میں سے ہوں

📖 What is Ayat e Karima?

Ayat e Karima is a powerful supplication from the Holy Quran, found in Surah Al-Anbiya (21:87). It is the dua of Prophet Yunus (A.S), who called upon Allah from the depths of darkness when he was inside the whale. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

This verse is one of the most profound duas in Islam because it combines Tawheed (Oneness of Allah), Tasbeeh (glorifying Allah), and Istighfar (seeking forgiveness) all in one short but powerful sentence. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

🐋 Story of Prophet Yunus (A.S)

Prophet Yunus (A.S) was sent to guide his people, but when they rejected his message, he left before Allah’s command. As a result, he faced a great trial and was swallowed by a whale. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Inside the whale, surrounded by three layers of darkness (night, sea, and the whale), he realized his mistake and turned to Allah with complete humility.

He recited this powerful dua (Ayat e Karima), and Allah accepted his repentance and saved him from distress. This event became a timeless lesson for all believers. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

🧠 Deep Meaning of Ayat e Karima

  • La ilaha illa anta: A declaration that only Allah has power over everything (Tawheed).
  • Subhanaka: Allah is perfect and free from all نقص (imperfection).
  • Inni kuntu minaz zalimeen: Admitting one's mistakes and seeking forgiveness.

This structure teaches us the perfect way to make dua: Praise Allah → Glorify Him → Admit your mistakes → Ask for help. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

🌙 Spiritual Importance

Ayat e Karima is not just a dua — it is a complete lesson in faith. It teaches humility, patience, and reliance on Allah.

Allah says in the Quran that just as He saved Yunus (A.S), He will save the believers as well — showing that this dua carries a universal promise for all Muslims. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

📿 How to Recite (Wazifa)

  • Recite daily (100, 125, or 313 times)
  • Best after Fajr, Tahajjud, or before sleep
  • Start with Durood Sharif
  • Make sincere intention (dua)
  • Recite with full focus and humility
  • Be consistent and patient

Consistency and sincerity are key to gaining its full benefits. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}
