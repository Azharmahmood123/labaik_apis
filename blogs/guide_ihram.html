🤍 IHRAM GUIDE إحرام

Sacred state · Garments of purity · For Hajj & Umrah

1. What is Ihram

#1

The dress worn by pilgrims is known as Ihram and generally consists of two white sheets.

The larger sheet is used to cover the body from ankles to the navel.

The other sheet must be draped around the shoulders.

2. When Ihram Is Worn?

#2

Ihram is worn by pilgrims while performing Hajj or Umraah.

It is mandatory for the pilgrims to wear Ihram at the place of Meqaat near Mecca in order to continue their pilgrimage.

3. Wajibat and Sunnah

#3
⚡ Wajibat of Ihram (Obligations)
  • Going in state of Ihram and wearing Ihram at Meqaat
  • Restrain from the things that are prohibited in Ihram
✨ Sunnahs of Ihram (Recommended Acts)
  • Performing ablution or ghusl
  • Applying it before making Niyyah for Ihram or either Hajj or Ummrah
  • Using two sheets as Ihram dress
  • Performing two Rakat of Sunnah of Ihram
  • Recitation of Talbiyah
  • Reciting loudly
  • Reciting thrice

4. Rules of Ihram

#4
  • Talbiyah must be said together with Niyyah for completion of ihram.
  • It is obligatory to recite talabiyah aloud for once
  • Talabivah can be said in minor or major impurity
  • The ghusl done in either day or night suffices for the rest of the day of ihram
  • The pilgrim can take off and replace the ihram during the pilgrimage
📘 Note: Talbiyah recitation, ghusl validity, and the flexibility of removing or replacing Ihram garments are key aspects. Pilgrims may change ihram garments if needed while maintaining the sacred state.

5. Things Forbidden in Ihram

#5
🚫 Prohibited acts & substances during Ihram
  • Hunting of land animals
  • Kissing a woman
  • Sexual intercourse
  • Touching a woman
  • Indulging in fore-play
  • Marriage
  • Masturbation
  • Use of perfume
  • Wearing sewn clothes (only for men)
  • Wearing socks or shoes (only for men)
  • Covering the head (only for men)
  • Shading from rain or sun (only for men)
  • Looking in the miror
  • Application of kohl on eyes
  • Quarreling
  • Cursing
  • Killing of insects on one's body
  • Application of oil on body
  • Use of cosmetics
  • Shaving bodily hair
  • Covering of face (for women)
  • Causing the flow of blood out from body
  • Pulling out of teeth
  • Clipping nails
