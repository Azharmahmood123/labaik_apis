1
Introduction to Umrah

Umrah in Islamic terminology means paying a visit to the Ka‘bah, performing Tawaf around it, and walking between Safaa and Marwah seven times. A performer of Umrah puts off their Ihram by having hair shaved or cut. Unlike Hajj, Umrah can be performed at any time of the year.

Pillars of Umrah (4)

  • Ihram: The sacred state and dress code assumed at the Meeqat.
  • Tawaf: Circumambulation around the Ka‘bah (7 circuits).
  • Sa'ee: Walking between As-Safaa and Al-Marwah (7 circuits).
  • Tahal-lul: Exiting the state of Ihram, making lawful what was previously prohibited.

During Umrah, pilgrims do not go to Mina, Arafat, Muzdalifah, or throw pebbles. Those rites are specific to Hajj.

2
Importance & Virtues

Umrah cleanses one’s soul from past sins and can be performed multiple times as an expiation. It protects from poverty and draws the mercy of Allah. The Prophet ﷺ said that performing Umrah in Ramadan equals Hajj in reward. Moreover, if a person passes away during the journey of Umrah, they are granted Jannah.

3
Preparation before the Journey
  • Learn about Makkah, Madinah, and Umrah rituals thoroughly.
  • Arrange accommodation, documents (passport, ID), and necessary funds.
  • Get to know travel companions; having an experienced pilgrim is beneficial.
  • Prepare emergency contacts, medications, and a list of Duas requested by friends/family.
  • Pack Ihram garments, personal hygiene items, and lightweight essentials.
4
The Ihram – Sacred Dress & State

Ihram is both the dress code and the intention (niyyah) by which a person enters the sacred state. Men wear two white seamless sheets; women maintain their ordinary modest clothing (no face-cover nor gloves). Once in Ihram, certain actions become forbidden: cutting nails, hair, using perfume, marital relations, hunting, and covering the head (for men).

5
Intention (Niyyah) & Talbiyah

Before crossing the Meeqat, perform 2 rak‘ahs of nafl (if not makruh time). After the salah, uncover your head (for men) and make the intention for Umrah:

اَلّلٰهُمَّ اِنِّيْ اُرِيْدُ الْعُمْرَةَ فَيَسِّرْهَا لِيْ وَتَقَبَّلْهَا مِنِّيْ

“O Allah, I intend to perform Umrah, make it easy for me and accept it from me.”

Then recite Talbiyah three times (men raise voice, women recite softly):

لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ والنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكُ ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ

“Here I am O Allah, here I am. You have no partner, here I am. Verily all praise, grace and sovereignty belong to You. You have no partner.”

Repeat Talbiyah frequently until beginning Tawaf. Then recite durood and ask Allah for Paradise and protection from Fire.

6
The Meeqat – Boundaries of Ihram

Meeqat are fixed stations beyond which pilgrims cannot pass without Ihram. The five locations designated by Prophet ﷺ:

  • Dhul Halayfah (for Madinah pilgrims) – 435 km from Makkah.
  • Al-Juhfah (Syria, Egypt, Jordan region) – 180 km from Makkah.
  • Yalamlam (Yemen side) – 92 km from Makkah.
  • Qarn Al Manaazil (Najd, Taif) – 75 km from Makkah.
  • Dhatu Irq (Iraq, Khurasan) – 100 km from Makkah.

For air travel, assume Ihram before the plane crosses the Meeqat boundary.

7
Entering Al-Masjid Al-Haram

Enter with your right foot, expressing humility and gratitude, and recite:

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي ذُنُوبِي وَافْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ

“My Lord, forgive my sins and open the doors of Your mercy for me.”

Upon seeing the Ka‘bah, stop reciting Talbiyah. Raise your hands in supplication.

8
Tawaf – Circumambulation of Ka‘bah

Begin at the Hajr-e-Aswad (Black Stone). Touch it with right hand, kiss it or, if not possible, point towards it and say “Bismillah, Allahu Akbar”. Keep the Ka‘bah on your left. Men do Idhtibaa‘ (right shoulder bare) and Raml (fast-paced walking with small steps) during first three circuits.

Recommended supplication during Tawaf:

سُبْحَانَ اللهِ، وَالْحَمْدُ للهِ، ولَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ، وَاللهُ أَكْبَرُ، وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللهِ العَلِيِّ الْعَظيِمِ

Between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone, recite:

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

“Our Lord, grant us good in this world and the Hereafter, and save us from the Fire.” (Qur’an 2:201)

After completing 7 circuits (making Istilam 8 times), pray 2 rak‘ahs behind Maqam Ibrahim (or anywhere in Haram), reciting Surah Al-Kafirun and Al-Ikhlas. Then drink Zamzam water with the dua:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمَاً نَافِعَاً وَرِزْقَاً وَاسِعَاً وَشِفَاءً مِنْ كُلِّ دَاءٍ

“O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, abundant provision, and cure from every disease.”

9
Sa'ee – Walking between Safa & Marwah

Proceed to Mount Safa. Face the Ka‘bah and recite three times:

اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

“Allah is the Greatest … none has the right to be worshipped except Allah alone, He fulfilled His promise, gave victory to His servant, and defeated the confederates alone.”

Walk towards Marwah. Between the two green fluorescent markers (mīlain akhḍarayn), men jog lightly. Make du'a and dhikr. Upon reaching Marwah, repeat the same supplication facing the Ka‘bah. This completes one lap. Repeat until you complete 7 laps, ending at Marwah.

10
Halq or Taqsir – Final Exit from Ihram

After completing Sa'ee, proceed to a barber (or do it yourself). Men should shave their head completely (Halq) or trim hair evenly (Taqsir); shaving is preferred. Women cut a fingertip's length (approx. 2.5 cm) from the end of their hair. Once this is done, the state of Ihram is lifted and all normal lawful acts become permissible. Umrah is now complete. Alhamdulillah!

May Allah accept your Umrah and bless your journey. You may optionally perform two rak‘ahs in the mataf area as a gesture of gratitude.
