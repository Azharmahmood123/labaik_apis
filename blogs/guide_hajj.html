1. Introduction#1

Hajj is an Arabic word carrying the meanings of heading for an honorable place or person. Hajj is among the five pillars of Islam in which Muslims perform pilgrimage to Mecca. Performing Hajj is obligatory for a Muslim who can afford and fulfills certain conditions.

2. Time and Place of Hajj#2

Time of Hajj
Hajj is performed in the Zil-Hajj; the last month of Islamic Calendar from the 8th to 13th day.

Umrah can be performed during any time in a year while Hajj is specific to these days only.

Place of Hajj
The Holy Mosque of Kabba, is located in Mecca, Saudi Arabia.

3. Who Must Perform Hajj#3

There are certain conditions which must be fulfilled by a Muslim to perform at least one Hajj in his or her lifetime.

The conditions are:

  • Mentally healthy and in total contract of his or her mental faculties.
  • Wise and mature enough to understand the importance of what he or she is about to do.
  • Financially sound not only in the financing the Hajj but also supporting the dependants during the absence.
4. Hajj Prerequisites#4

Hajj requires complete devotion and peace of mind, fulfilling the prerequisites one can achieve the peace of mind and devotion required.

The prerequisites includes:

  • The intention of performing Hajj should be to please Allah only and not to gain fame or impress people.
  • The Hajj expenses should be paid from legitimate (Halal) money. Money from any other means is not acceptable.
  • All the debts and financial liabilities should be cleared before setting off for the journey.
  • Based on the instructions of Prophet (PBUH) one must resolve all the conflicts with other people or ask for forgiveness from other people whom they may have hurt in the past before going to Hajj.
5. Preparing for Hajj#5
  • Ask for the help of Allah to assist in resources for Hajj.
  • Inform your employer about the vacations well before consulting with the travel agent.
  • Search for a travel agent before visiting the marketplace and consulting with other Hajjis.
  • From your travel agent get information about all the legal requirements and start fulfilling them.
  • Start learning about the rituals of Hajj with help of different books.
  • Pay proper attention to your physical toil from a person.
  • Meet people who have performed Hajj and try to get an insight about the situation and circumstance during Hajj.
6. Fundamental Acts of Hajj#6

The Fundamental acts include:

  • Entering the state of Ihram
  • Standing in the ground of Arafat
  • Tawaaf of Ka'aba
  • Sa'yi (the act of hastening between the hills of Safa and Marwa)

Not being able to perform these acts results in the Hajj not being complete and the person must perform it again.

7. Compulsory Acts of Hajj#7

The compulsory acts include:

  • Wearing of Ihram from the Miqat
  • Spending a day in the field of Arafat
  • Spending night in Muzdalefah
  • Stay at Mina
  • Casting at Jamarat
  • Head shaving or hair shortening
  • Farewell Tawaaf of Ka'aba
  • Activities performed in the days of Hajj

Missing any of the compulsory acts of Hajj makes it mandatory for the person to sacrifice an animal against every act missed.

8. Days of Hajj#8

1st Day: On 8th Zil-Hajj in the morning after wearing of Ihram, people travel from Mecca to Mina.

2nd Day: On 9th Zil-Hajj people arrive at Arafat early in the morning where they stay till Asar Salah performing Ibadah and leave for Muzdalifah before the sunset.

3rd Day: On 10th Zil-Hajj people stay the whole day in Muzdalfah where they say their Maghrib and Isha prayers together and perform Ibadah the whole night long. After offering the Fajar prayer the pilgrims leave for Mina where at first they make sacrifice (Qurbani), shave or trim their hair and remove the Ihram. Afterwards, the Tawaaf-e-Ziyaarah is performed and then the Sa'yi between Safa and Marwa and then the pilgrims return to Mina.

4th Day: On 11th Zil-Hajj the three Jamraats are stoned.

5th Day: On 12th Zil-Hajj the three Jamraats are again stoned and the pilgrims perform the Farewell Tawaaf before leaving Mecca.

9. Hajj in a Glance#9
📌 Overview: From 8th Dhul Hijjah to 13th — Ihram, Mina, Arafat, Muzdalifah, Rami, Sacrifice, Tawaf al-Ifada, Sa'yi, Farewell Tawaf. A spiritual marathon of devotion and unity.

Essential pillars: Ihram, standing at Arafat, Tawaf, Sa'yi. Missing any requires repetition. The days of Tashreeq involve remembrance of Allah and stoning the Jamarat. Perform with sincerity and knowledge.

10. Hajj Day 1 (8th Dhul Hijjah) - Entering Ihram#10
  • Take a Bath (Ghusl)
  • Wear Ihram Dress
  • Make intention for Umrah or Hajj
  • Recite Talbiyah
  • Avoid Unnecessary Acts while in the State of Ihram
11. Hajj Day 1 (8th Dhul Hijjah) - Performing Umrah#11
  • Perform Tawaaf around the Ka'bah
  • Offer two Rak'ah Salat behind Place of Ibrahim
  • Between Safa and Marwah, Make Sa'yi
  • Cut your hair and remove Ihram dress
12. Hajj Day 1 (8th Dhul Hijjah) - Departure to Mina#12
  • After Umrah, wear Ihram dress again
  • Make intention for Hajj
  • Stay in Mina during the 8th of Zhul-Hijja and perform five prayers (Zuhr prayer to Fajr prayer on the day of Arafat)
13. Hajj Day 2 (9th Dhul Hijjah) Departure to Arafat#13
  • Going for Arafat on the morning of the 9th of Zhul-Hijja and stay there until sunset
  • Reside in any part of Arafat
  • Glorify Allah, repeat supplication, repent to Allah and ask for forgiveness
  • Pray Zuhr and Asar Salat combined at the time of Zuhr Salat
14. Hajj Day 2 (9th Dhul Hijjah) – Departure to Muzdalifah#14
  • Leave for Muzdalifah shortly after sunset on the 9th of Zhul-Hijja
  • Offer Maghrib Salat and shortened Isha salat combined
  • Now stay all night and offer the Fajr prayer
15. Hajj Day 3 (10th Dhul Hijjah) - Returning to Mina#15
  • Soon before sunrise, leave Muzdalifah for Mina. Depart to Jamarat al-Agabah and stone it with seven pebbles
  • Slaughter your sacrifice
  • Shave your head
  • Now take off Ihram dress
16. Hajj Day 3 (10th Dhul Hijjah)– Tawaf al-Ifada#16
  • Perform Tawaaf al-Ifada and Sa'yi between Safa and Marwah
  • After Tawaf al-Ifada, all restrictions are removed
17. Hajj Day 4,5 (11th & 12th Dhul Hijjah) - Returning to Mina#17
  • Spend the Tashreeq days (11th, 12th, and 13th of Zhul-Hijja) in Mina
  • After Zuhr prayer each day, stone the three Jamarat, starting with the small one and ending with al-Acjabah
18. Farewell Tawaf#18
  • Depart to Mecca and do a farewell Tawaf. (Pray two Rakat Salat)
  • Let the Tawaaf be the last thing you do in Mecca
19. Return to Home#19

It is a preferred step to visit the Prophet's Mosque in Medina, but this is not a part of Hajj.

May Allah accept your pilgrimage and grant you a safe return.
